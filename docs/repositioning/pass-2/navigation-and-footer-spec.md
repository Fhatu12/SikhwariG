# Navigation and Footer Spec

## Desktop Navigation

Keep primary navigation compact:

- Home
- Services
- About
- Contact

Do not add all four division pages as top-level nav items. Use Services and footer links for deeper navigation.

## Mobile Navigation

Reuse the current menu-button pattern. Mobile menu should include:

- Home
- Services
- About
- Contact
- Request a quote

Keep touch targets at least 44px high.

## Footer Direction

Footer should support:

- legal identity block
- registered address publicly displayed
- company email: `info@sikhwarigroup.co.za`
- service links grouped by the two portfolios
- legal links: Privacy, Terms, Disclaimer
- exact branding: `Built by SG Digital | A division of Sikhwari Group (Pty) Ltd`

Do not publish personal contact details.

Later implementation should update route metadata to include hospitality and diversified services. Sitemap should continue to include existing public division routes and exclude admin/API routes.
