# About and Leadership Design Spec

## Revised About Structure

| Section | Purpose | Design treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| About hero | State one legal entity and diversified service model | Existing `Section` pattern with concise copy |
| Internal division model | Explain SG Telecoms, SG Digital, SG Cyber and SG Hospitality | Two portfolio cards with division lists |
| Company structure | Clarify portfolios and four public service areas | Compact table or cards |
| Leadership | Show accountability without over-design | Two restrained cards |
| Qualifications | Display approved degrees/diplomas only | Short bullet list inside each leader card or below it |
| Delivery principles | Trust and operating model | 3-4 short principles: documented scope, accountability, disciplined execution, authorised work |
| Proof/trust | Show redacted proof only where approved | Existing `ProofSection`, cautious heading |
| Selected Work | Show approved work cards | Reusable Selected Work component |

## Leadership Presentation

Use the approved titles exactly in visible copy:

| Leader | Title treatment | Portfolio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fhatuwani Sikhwari | Group CEO / Managing Director | Technology, Telecommunications and Cybersecurity Portfolio |
| Tendani Sikhwari | Executive Director: Operations, Hospitality and Corporate Services | Operations, Hospitality and Corporate Services |

Keep cards simple: avatar/photo, name, title, portfolio, short role summary and approved qualifications. Do not use oversized biography cards or imply unaudited accreditations.

## Qualification Rule

Degrees and diplomas may be displayed when approved. Do not expose ID numbers, private emails, private phone numbers, residential/private information, reference contact details or unnecessary certificate metadata.

## Content Tone

Use factual, restrained wording. Avoid heroic claims, guaranteed outcomes and unsupported procurement or accreditation language.
