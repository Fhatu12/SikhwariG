# Pass 2 UI Handoff

## Verdict

Pass 2 defines a minimal-change UI/UX handoff for repositioning Sikhwari Group (Pty) Ltd as a diversified South African services company. It preserves the current design language, routes, stack, enquiry workflow, admin protections, legal routes and proprietary-trading disclaimer.

## Design Direction

- Keep the quiet corporate UI: teal actions, slate text, white cards, soft borders and concise sections.
- Reposition the homepage around one accountable South African company, two portfolios and four public service areas.
- Make hospitality visible on homepage, services, contact and footer.
- Keep top-level navigation compact.
- Add Selected Work as a reusable card section for approved projects.
- Extend the contact form only in a later engineering slice with coordinated backend/privacy changes.

## Non-Negotiables

- Do not publish Proprietary Trading and Treasury as a public service or enquiry option.
- Do not display the B-BBEE/ownership certificate publicly.
- Do not expose ID numbers, private contact details, residential/private information, reference contacts or unnecessary certificate metadata.
- Do not use unsupported procurement, licensing, accreditation, ISP, spectrum, financial-services or guaranteed-outcome claims.
- Keep registered address publicly displayed as approved.
- Use `info@sikhwarigroup.co.za` as the public privacy contact.
