# Pass 1 Summary

## Verdict

The current website is a stable Next.js corporate site with preserved lead capture, notification, admin, legal and security foundations. It is not yet aligned to the approved repositioning because homepage, footer and metadata still emphasise three technology-oriented services and omit hospitality. The services page and form already include the four approved public service areas. Proprietary trading is restricted to the legal disclaimer and admin guardrail context, not public service routing.

## Key Findings

- Baseline branch was `master` at `3be0a5b`, aligned with `origin/master`.
- Existing untracked files `npm-audit.json` and `npm-audit.after.json` were present before this pass and were left untouched.
- Public routes include home, about, services, contact, four division pages, privacy, terms, disclaimer, sitemap and robots.
- Protected admin routes include leads, services, posts and proof.
- Production-sensitive capabilities include lead database storage, SMTP notification, anti-spam/rate-limit controls, admin authentication, no-store admin handling, security headers and legal disclaimer blocks.
- Homepage and footer omit hospitality.
- Services and contact form already include Culinary and Hospitality Services.
- About leadership titles are narrower than the approved working titles.
- Selected Work currently publishes only Mzansi Select.
- Proof claims are database-driven and require source documents before stronger public use.
- Privacy wording needs legal review for responsible-party, contact, operator, database, SMTP, retention and complaint details.
- Legal update dates are `February 24, 2026` and need review against company registration and approval records.

## Supported Claims

- One legal entity with service lines/divisions.
- Four public service areas exist in service content and form routing.
- Cybersecurity work is framed as authorised/advisory/assessment/support.
- Proprietary trading is internal-only, company-owned-funds-only and not a public solicitation.
- Lead capture and notification capabilities are implemented in code.

## Prohibited Or Unsupported Claims

- Proprietary Trading and Treasury as a public service.
- Investment manager or financial-services provider.
- Licensed network operator, internet-service provider or spectrum licence holder.
- Accredited cybersecurity provider or certified software-development company.
- Government approved/preferred, tender-ready, fully CSD compliant or fully verified supplier.
- Guaranteed delivery or security outcomes.
- B-BBEE, ownership and black female ownership claims without source documents.

## Missing Evidence

- B-BBEE certificate and expiry.
- Ownership source documents.
- Leadership qualifications/certifications.
- Current proof item sources.
- CSD/tax/procurement records.
- Selected Work approvals and source artefacts for Mzansi Select, V-Property, SG Digital Trust Check, Tendi's Hair & Beauty and the corporate website.
